Subject: want a driver for the Apple SMC (temperatures, fans) on Intel Macs

Intel Macs keep their temperatures, fan speeds, voltages and power values in the System Management Controller (SMC). illumos has no driver for it, so on a Mac Pro or iMac only the CPU and chipset sensors (coretemp, pchtemp) are visible; GPU, memory, display, power supply and fan values are not, and FMA sees none of them.

h3. Proposal

A small read-only driver, applesmc(4D), x86 only:

* finds the SMC through the ACPI device APP0001 and its I/O port window (normally 0x300-0x31f), as Linux's drivers/hwmon/applesmc.c and FreeBSD's asmc(4) do; on machines without APP0001 it does not attach;
* never writes keys: fan and thermal control stay with the SMC;
* registers every temperature key of type sp78 or flt that reads a plausible value as a ksensor (@/dev/sensors/temperature/smc/<key>@), so the values are available to FMA and any ksensor consumer;
* offers every key through @/dev/applesmc@ with four ioctls in @<sys/applesmc.h>@ (count, key by index, key info, read), raw as the SMC sends them;
* plus smcstat(8): temperatures and fans; @-p@ parsable, @-a@ all keys, @-i@ interval, or the given keys.

The devfsadm rule for @/dev/applesmc@ goes into misc_link_i386.c; the package is driver/sensor/applesmc (depends on system/ksensor); man pages applesmc.4d and smcstat.8.

h3. Testing

* Mac Pro (Late 2013, MacPro6,1), OpenIndiana 2026.10 (illumos 586678ac1b): 78 temperature ksensors; fan F0 "Main", 790-1900 rpm.
* iMac (21.5-inch, Late 2013, iMac14,1), OpenIndiana 2026.10 (illumos 08e59b676b): 37 temperature ksensors; fan F0 "Main", 1400-2800 rpm.
* On both: built out of tree as a package and installed with pkg; @/dev/applesmc@ and the ksensors appear at install, smcstat reads the temperatures and fans.
* A GTK application that uses @smcstat -p@ and the ksensors: https://github.com/isoriano1968/mac-sensors

h3. Open points for review

* The @/dev/applesmc@ interface is private (Uncommitted in the man page); it exists for smcstat and similar tools.
* On iMac14,1 TA0P reads exactly 20 °C below TA0p (raw vs. corrected ambient sensor); both are exported as they are.
* Some private SMC keys refuse reads (EIO); @smcstat -a@ reports them.
